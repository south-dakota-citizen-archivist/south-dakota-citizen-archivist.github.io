    Where this comes from

    The South Dakota Secretary of State publishes the number of registered voters in each county by party: monthly since December 2015 on its voter registration totals page, and for each election from 1976 to 2014 in its election history. Each report here links to the Secretary of State's file and to a copy kept with this site.

    How it's built

    A new report is checked for every day. Each one is read from its PDF by its column headings (the parties change order from report to report) and checked before it's used: 66 counties, every party column adding up to the report's total, and each county's parties adding up to its total of active voters. A report that fails a check isn't added. The reports from 1976 to 2014, and a few monthly reports from 2015 to 2018 whose layout can't be read reliably, were entered by hand from the reports.

    • Counties are matched to their Census codes as of each report's date. Shannon County became Oglala Lakota County in 2015; here its earlier figures are shown as Oglala Lakota's. Washabaugh County, which joined Jackson County in 1983, is left out of the Republican-Democratic-other view; some early reports count it with Jackson.
    • The September 1, 2026 report was first published with its Republican and Democratic columns switched; the Secretary of State replaced it on September 14, and the corrected figures are used. Both files are kept.
    • The figures for the 2014 general election carry no date in their source; they're shown as October 20, 2014, two weeks before the election.
